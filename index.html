Sidekick Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Sidekick is a browser extension that helps users identify the sports match they are currently watching on supported streaming websites and provides the relevant Sidekick experience for that match.

Information Sidekick Uses

Sidekick may access information from the currently viewed supported webpage that is necessary to identify the sports match being watched. This may include webpage information such as the page URL, page title, or relevant match information available on the page.

Sidekick may also store extension preferences and settings using Chrome's extension storage functionality.

How We Use Information

Information accessed by Sidekick is used only to provide and operate Sidekick's core functionality, including identifying the current sports match and providing the appropriate match-related experience.

We do not use information accessed by Sidekick for unrelated purposes.

Information Sent to Third Parties

When necessary to provide match-detection functionality, Sidekick may transmit the limited information required for that functionality to services used by Sidekick.

Sidekick does not sell users' personal information.

Data Storage

Sidekick may store extension settings and preferences locally using Chrome's extension storage APIs.

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the relevant functionality or as otherwise required for legitimate operational purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by Sidekick. Internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Sidekick is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Sidekick's functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
